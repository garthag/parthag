Your data

Everything stays in your own Google account. The app keeps a small Google Sheet with the details it detected (document type, number, amount, text it read, your corrections). Files you save go to the Drive folder Mail Attachments.

Open data sheet Open "Mail Attachments" in Drive

Security

  • Runs as you, inside Google. No password is typed or stored anywhere.
  • Gmail access is read-only. The app cannot delete, move or change your mail.
  • Drive access is limited to files this app creates. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Sending uses Google's send-only permission, and only when you press Share.
  • Remove access any time: myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps.

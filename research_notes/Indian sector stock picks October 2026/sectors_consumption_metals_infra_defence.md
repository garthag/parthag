## 1. FMCG & consumption: rural vs urban, GST cuts, festive 2026

### Takeaway
Underlying FMCG volumes were weak in Apr-Jun 2026: down 2% overall, with rural volumes down 5%. Q2 FY27 results so far are mixed. HUL posted its best underlying sales growth in 14 quarters, but margins fell 140 bp on commodity costs. Nestle grew revenue about 20%, but the stock fell on results day. Discretionary and quick-commerce businesses (Titan, Eternal) are growing much faster than staples.

### Cited Findings
- **Sector volumes:** NielsenIQ data for Apr-Jun 2026 shows FMCG volumes down 2% YoY. Value rose 0.8%, helped by 2.8% price increases, and 68% of categories saw volume declines. Rural volumes fell 5% while urban was flat (+0.1%). — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/fmcg-slowdown-is-splitting-indias-consumer-basket)
- **Festive outlook:** Industry estimates put festive-season FMCG demand up 9-11%. Datum Intelligence expects festive online sales of ₹1.50-1.55 lakh crore, driven by more orders rather than bigger baskets. Both are projections, and I found no measured post-festive data. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/fmcg-slowdown-is-splitting-indias-consumer-basket); [Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/22699/absorbing-costs-protecting-demand-inside-the-festive-fmcg-pricing-strategy)
- **GST 2.0 effect:** The GST 2.0 cuts (Sept 2025) gave volumes only a short-lived boost. In the Sep-2025 quarter, volume growth slowed to 5.4% amid GST-transition disruption. The 2026 festive season is framed as the real test of whether demand from the cuts lasts. — [Business Standard (Oct 2025)](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/fmcg-sector-diwali-sales-demand-growth-gst-cuts-quick-commerce-125101700104_1.html); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/fmcg-slowdown-is-splitting-indias-consumer-basket)

## 2. Metals & mining: global prices, China exports, safeguard duty

### Takeaway
Domestic steel is protected by a 3-year safeguard duty (12% falling to 11%). Mills raised HRC prices for three straight months to October, but prices flattened in late October as buyers pushed back, and coking-coal costs are squeezing per-tonne margins. Non-ferrous producers are in a much stronger position: zinc and silver are well above FY26 averages, and Hindustan Zinc's Q2 profit rose 142%.

### Cited Findings
- **Safeguard duty:** The duty is 12% until April 2026, 11.5% until April 2027 and 11% until April 2028, on flat steel imports mainly from China, Vietnam and Nepal. Antique (Jan 2026) named Tata Steel and Jindal Steel as top picks on the back of it. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/govt-imposes-up-to-12-safeguard-duty-on-steel-flat-product-imports-125123100448_1.html); [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/safeguard-duty-tata-steel-jindal-steel-top-metal-stock-picks-says-antique-508996-2026-01-01)

## 3. Power & renewables: demand growth, auctions, transmission

### Takeaway
Power demand was very strong in September 2026: peak demand of about 269 GW versus 229 GW a year earlier, and roughly 11% consumption growth. Evening shortages were the worst since August 2023. October has been softer because of rains. NTPC's Q2 generation rose 13%. Adani Green delivered high growth but carries leverage of 5.6x net debt to EBITDA. Waaree's revenue grew while its profit fell, and it holds a large order book.

### Cited Findings
- **September 2026 demand:**
  - Peak demand was 269.06 GW versus 229.15 GW in Sep 2025.
  - Average consumption was about 5,408 MU/day versus about 4,863 MU.
  - Unmet demand of about 0.3% was the highest since Aug 2023, mostly after sunset.
  - Source: [LEAP Journal blog, Oct 2026](https://blog.theleapjournal.org/2026/10/supply-stress-and-shortfalls-how-india.html); consumption up 11.35% to 162.19 BU per [Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/energy-infrastructure/power-and-renewable-energy/indias-power-consumption-rises-11.35-per-cent-to-162.19-bu/98313)
- **October 2026 demand:** One report says demand fell 5.2% in October on unseasonal rains. A secondary analysis has the peak easing from about 260 GW (5 Oct) to about 234 GW (26 Oct). — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/indias-power-demand-slips-5-2-in-october-amid-unseasonal-rains-report/amp); [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/analysis/india-power-demand-coal-stress-prices-october-2026/)
- **NTPC Q2 FY27 operating data:**
  - Generation was 117.9 BU, up 12.93% YoY.
  - Coal dispatch rose 28.45% to 11.899 Mt.
  - Trading volume was 15.06 BU, up 22%.
  - Installed capacity is over 91 GW, with about 35% more under construction.
  - No financial results were found.
  - Source: [ANI, 1 Oct 2026](https://aninews.in/news/business/ntpc-power-generation-rises-nearly-13-in-q2-coal-dispatch-power-trading-also-jump20261001132004/); [Indian Masterminds](https://indianmasterminds.com/news/ntpc-q2-fy27-power-generation-117-9-bu-coal-dispatch-trading-growth-238245/)
- **Adani Green Q2 FY27:**
  - PAT was ₹747 cr, up about 16%, and revenue was ₹4,258 cr, up 41.6%.
  - EBITDA was ₹3,797 cr, up about 46-51%; the sources differ.
  - The operating portfolio is 20.8 GW.
  - **Net debt to run-rate EBITDA is 5.6x.**
  - Source: [Finology](https://ticker.finology.in/discover/market-update/adani-green-energy-q2-fy27-results); [indmoney](https://www.indmoney.com/blog/stocks/adani-green-energy-q2-results)
- **Waaree Energies Q2 FY27:**
  - Revenue was ₹7,518 cr, up 24%.
  - Operating EBITDA was ₹1,335 cr, a 17.8% margin.
  - PAT fell about 14-15%, to ₹718.68 cr (BW) or ₹753.64 cr (other sources); the figures conflict, likely attributable versus consolidated.
  - Depreciation rose from ₹240 cr to ₹347 cr.
  - FY27 EBITDA guidance of ₹7,000-7,700 cr was reaffirmed.
  - The order book was about ₹59,200 cr.
  - Source: [BW Businessworld](https://www.businessworld.in/article/waaree-energies-q2-profit-falls-15-to-rs-719-cr-despite-24-revenue-growth-630778); [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/blog/waaree-energies-q2-fy27-results-revenue-rises-24-indian-rupee7518-crore-order-book-indian-rupee59200-crore); [ScanX](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/waaree-energies-net-profit-falls-14-754-crore-q2fy27/54657625)

### Inferences
- **Peak and evening shortages favour storage, transmission and thermal baseload players.** Indicative views:
  - **NTPC: Buy/accumulate.** Generation is growing, the capex pipeline is large and it has regulated returns.
  - **Power Grid: Hold/Buy.** Transmission build-out beneficiary, but no data was gathered.
  - **Adani Green: Avoid/high-risk.** Strong growth, but 5.6x leverage plus Adani-group governance overhang. Promoter pledge was not verified.
  - **Waaree: Hold.** Profit is falling as solar module prices and margins normalise; this is the key risk for Waaree and Premier.
- **Suzlon, JSW Energy, NHPC, Tata Power, Premier Energies:** no data was gathered.

## 4. Infra & capital goods: government capex, order books

### Takeaway
L&T posted a record Q2 FY27 order inflow of ₹1.48 lakh crore (+28%), driven by international hydrocarbon orders (63% international). Its order book is ₹8.54 lakh crore, but domestic infrastructure orders fell 13% because select orders were deferred. That points to a softer domestic public-capex ordering cycle. BHEL's profit doubled, but its order inflow was muted.

### Cited Findings
- **L&T Q2 FY27** (27 Oct 2026):
  - Order inflow was ₹1,47,665 cr, up 28%; international orders were ₹92,375 cr (63%).
  - The order book was ₹8,53,514 cr, up about 15% versus March 2026.
  - H1 inflow was ₹2,55,678 cr, up 22%.
  - Energy-Conventional orders were about ₹78,413 cr, more than double. Infrastructure & Utilities orders were about ₹35,509 cr, down 13% on deferrals.
  - PAT was ₹5,202 cr, up 33%, and revenue was ₹78,750 cr, up 16%, beating estimates.
  - EBITDA margin was 8.7% versus 10.0% a year earlier.
  - The stock rose up to 6.2% intraday to ₹3,894 on 28 Oct 2026.
  - Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/lt-q2-fy27-slides-record-orders-hit-15-trillion-margins-compress-93CH-4976353); [Zee Business](https://www.zeebiz.com/companies/news-lt-q2-fy27-results-net-profit-rises-33-yoy-to-rs-5202-crore-revenue-up-16-beats-estimates-404637); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/stocks/lt-q2-fy-27-results-share-price-in-focus/); [TradingView/Mint](https://www.tradingview.com/news/moodys:9044233a27acd:0-l-t-share-price-jumps-6-after-strong-q2-results-2026-should-you-buy-sell-or-hold-check-target-upside/)
- **BHEL Q2 FY27:**
  - Net profit rose about 120%.
  - The order book was ₹2,57,571 cr at 30 Sep 2026, but Q2 inflow was only about ₹4,000 cr.
  - Business Standard flags valuation concerns despite better execution and margins.
  - Sources: [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks/bhel-share-price-in-focus-reports-120-rise-in-q2-fy27-net-profit); [Univest](https://univest.in/blogs/for-bhel-a-muted-order-inflow-upsets-apple-cart-what-it-means-for-the-stock-after-q2-fy27-results); [Business Standard 16 Oct 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/better-execution-margin-gains-positive-for-bhel-despite-valuation-concerns-126101601260_1.html)

### Inferences
- **L&T: Buy.** Record orders, a 33% profit beat and about 3x book-to-bill visibility, offset by margin compression and dependence on Middle East hydrocarbon work (geopolitical risk given the cited West Asia conflict).
- **BHEL: Hold/avoid at current valuation.** The order book is huge, but inflows have stalled.
- The fall in domestic infrastructure orders suggests the government-led capex cycle is maturing. Unlike 2003-08, when private capex joined in, private-sector ordering must pick up for the cycle to extend. This is an inference, not sourced.

- No L&T consensus target, no central capex budget-utilisation data for FY27 H1, and no valuations or P/E.

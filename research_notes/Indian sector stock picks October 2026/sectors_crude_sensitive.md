# Crude-oil-sensitive Indian sectors and stock picks (as of 29 Oct 2026)

> IMPORTANT FRAMING NOTE FOR THE REPORT WRITER: The brief implies a soft-crude setup in which OMCs, paints, aviation and tyres benefit. The evidence says the opposite. **In October 2026 crude is HIGH, about $105-115/bbl Brent.** The cause is a US/Israel-Iran conflict that began 28 Feb 2026 and a near-closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Brent peaked at $138 in April 2026. Consumers of crude (OMC marketing, paints, aviation, tyres) are under margin pressure. Refiners are earning very high GRMs, and upstream producers gain unless the government taxes or caps them.

## Q1. Crude in Oct 2026: who benefits, who loses (margin data: GRMs, marketing margins, ATF)

### Takeaway
Brent is about $115 (28 Oct 2026) because of the Iran war and disruption at Hormuz. Integrated refiner-marketers (HPCL, BPCL, IOC, RIL O2C) are earning record GRMs (HPCL $32/bbl in Q2 FY27), but these are partly offset by marketing losses on diesel, petrol and LPG. Upstream (ONGC, Oil India) benefits from high realisations, and no windfall tax has been reported for 2026. A royalty cut actually helped them. ATF is up about 25% in three months, which hurts aviation. Paints and tyres face input inflation.

### Cited Findings
**Crude level and cause**
- Brent rose to $114.83/bbl on 28 Oct 2026 — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil)
- Brent was $115.22 on 20 Oct 2026. The 52-week range was $59.93 (16 Dec 2025) to $138.21 (7 Apr 2026) — [Macrotrends](https://www.macrotrends.net/2480/brent-crude-oil-prices-10-year-daily-chart)
- Brent was about $105.7 on 13 Oct 2026. This conflicts with the ~$115 readings above, which come from a later date — [Acumen](https://acumengroup.in/brent-crude-price/)
- Oil went above $100 after a near-closure of the Strait of Hormuz, which carries about 40% of India's crude imports. The trigger was US and Israeli strikes on Iran from 28 Feb 2026 — [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/worldbiz/asia/india-cuts-excise-duties-petrol-diesel-iran-war-drives-oil-shock-1395186); [Gulf Times](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/722964/international/india/india-cuts-excise-duties-on-petrol-diesel-as-global-oil-prices-surge)

**Government response (March 2026)**
- Special excise duty on petrol was cut from ₹13 to ₹3 per litre, and on diesel from ₹10 to zero. Pump prices were kept roughly unchanged, so the cut mainly relieved OMCs, which were then losing about ₹48.8/litre — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/govt-cuts-excise-duty-on-petrol-and-diesel-but-pump-prices-will-remain-the-same-heres-why-3946274); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/why-india-cut-fuel-excise-duty-now-petrol-prices-impact-iran-us-israel-war-126032700284_1.html)
- Export taxes were added: ₹21.5/litre on diesel exports and ₹29.5/litre on ATF exports. This matters for RIL's export-heavy refinery — [Gulf Times](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/722964/international/india/india-cuts-excise-duties-on-petrol-diesel-as-global-oil-prices-surge)

**OMC marketing margins and under-recoveries**
- Around 9 Sep 2026, with Brent above $100 and the Indian basket at about $109, OMCs were estimated to lose ₹5/litre on petrol and ₹23/litre on diesel. This is about 7 weeks old and crude has risen since — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/omcs-may-be-losing-5-litre-on-petrol-23-on-diesel-as-brent-crosses-100-126090901765_1.html)
- Baseline only: Nomura put average marketing margins at ₹6.8/litre in Q2 FY26 and ₹10.4/litre in Q1 FY26 — [Business Standard (Sep 2025)](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/crude-slips-to-3-month-low-inr-weakness-seen-pressuring-omcs-cgds-nomura-mahanagar-gas-ril-bpcl-hpcl-iocl-125091800172_1.html)
- I found no October 2026 marketing-margin figure (see Gaps).

**Refining margins (GRMs)**
- HPCL's Q2 FY27 GRM was $32.15/bbl, against $8.80 in Q2 FY26 — [Investing.com transcript summary](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/earnings-call-transcript-hpcl-posts-sharp-q2-turnaround-as-shares-fall-33-93CH-4898603)

**ATF**
- Delhi ATF rose from about ₹110/litre in July to about ₹137/litre on 1 Oct 2026, roughly +25% in three months. ATF rose more than 13% on 1 Oct 2026 alone. IndiGo raised fuel surcharges from 6 Oct — [Aerohaber IndiGo preview](https://aerohaber.com/indigo-q2-preview-can-higher-airfares-protect-margins-as-fuel-costs-surge/); [Google Finance INDIGO](https://www.google.com/finance/quote/INDIGO:NSE)

**Upstream**
- In late March 2026, no windfall tax had been announced on domestic crude. ONGC was at ₹287.90 and Oil India at ₹492.35 on the BSE, each up about 2%. Brokers said the market was pricing only about $65/bbl net realisation, reflecting fears of a cap — [Business Standard (30 Mar 2026)](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/ongc-oil-india-up-2-in-weak-market-stocks-rally-up-to-11-in-2-weeks-126033000257_1.html)
- In May 2026 the government made a surprise royalty cut on crude and gas production, easing windfall-tax fears. Oil India rose 7.5% intraday to ₹491 — [Invezz (12 May 2026)](https://invezz.com/en-ae/news/2026/05/12/ongc-and-oil-india-shares-soar-after-surprise-royalty-cut-by-government/)
- ONGC was ₹229.85 on 27 Oct 2026 according to a low-quality tracking site. If true, it is well below the March level despite higher crude. Unverified — [search summary of trackk.in / target sites](https://trackk.in/blog/ongc-share-price-target/)

### Inferences
- The brief's "who benefits / who loses" assumption is inverted for Oct 2026. Crude near $115 means:
  - **Upstream gains on realisations.** Their stocks have still underperformed, likely because of fears of a cap or levy and the ~$65 implied realisation.
  - **OMCs are mixed.** Huge GRMs are offset by diesel and LPG marketing losses.
  - **Paints, tyres and aviation lose on input costs.**
  - **Chemicals are mixed**, depending on pass-through.
- The ONGC price of ~₹230 (27 Oct) against ₹288 (30 Mar) needs verification. If correct, it suggests the market expects government capture of upside, or a reversal in crude.
- The thesis is very sensitive to a ceasefire or a Hormuz reopening. A sharp fall in crude would flip the winners and losers quickly: OMCs, paints and aviation would rally and upstream would fall.

### Inferences
These are unsourced background views; verify before use.
- **2014-16 crude crash:** OMCs (HPCL, BPCL, IOC) rerated sharply after diesel deregulation (Oct 2014) and as marketing margins expanded. Paints (Asian, Berger) and tyres also rerated on lower input costs. ONGC and Oil India underperformed.
